# YouTube Trending Video Analytics Dashboard





## Executive Summary:

This project analyzes YouTube trending video data to understand viewer engagement, content performance, and regional trends. The analysis focuses on four countries: India (IN), United States (US), Japan (JP), and South Korea (KR). An interactive Power BI dashboard was built to visualize key insights and trends.



---



## Business Problem:

The main business problem is understanding what type of YouTube content performs well across different countries. Brands and creators need insights into user engagement, video popularity, and trending patterns to improve their content strategy and maximize reach.



---



## Methodology:



### The project followed these steps:

\- Data collection from Kaggle (YouTube Trending Dataset)

\- Cleaning and preprocessing data using Python

\- Mapping category IDs using JSON files

\- Feature engineering such as engagement score and trending days

\- Data visualization using Power BI

\- Dashboard design and formatting



---



## Skills Used:

---



## Results \& Business Recommendations:

### Results:

\- Music and Entertainment are the most viewed categories

\- India and the US have the highest video views

\- Engagement patterns vary significantly by country



### Business Recommendations:

\- Content creators should invest more in high-performing categories

\- Brands should focus marketing campaigns on regions with higher engagement

\- Use data insights to optimize video titles and thumbnails
