# Simple Sales Dashboard Design:





## Title:



Simple Sales Dashboard Design — Superstore Sales Analysis



## Executive Summary:



This project focuses on designing an interactive sales dashboard using Power BI to visualize sales and profit performance across products, regions, and customer segments. The goal was to simplify business data into meaningful insights through effective visual storytelling. The dashboard allows users to identify key sales trends, high-performing categories, and segment-based sales contributions.



## Business Problem:



The company needs a clear and visual representation of its sales data to understand performance patterns. The challenge was to identify which months, product categories, and customer segments contributed most to overall revenue and profit. Without a visual tool, it’s difficult to make quick, data-driven business decisions.



## Methodology:



Imported Superstore\_Sales.csv dataset into Power BI.



Performed basic data cleaning (formatted dates, removed blanks, verified data types).



## Created visuals:



Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend



Donut Chart – Sales by Segment



Bar Chart – Sales and Profit by Category



Added slicers for interactive filtering (e.g., Region or Category).



Highlighted top-performing areas using color emphasis.

## Results \& Business Recommendations:



### Key Insights:



December and September recorded the highest sales peaks, indicating strong end-of-year demand.



The Consumer segment generated the majority of total sales, followed by Corporate and Home Office.



Office Supplies produced the highest profit, while Technology maintained stable performance.



### Overall Sales: 2.3M | Overall Profit: 286.4K



### Recommendations:



Focus marketing efforts on the Consumer segment.



Increase inventory for high-selling months (September–December).



Expand profit margins in low-performing categories like Furniture through pricing or promotions.
